Sample Itineraries

Three proven weekend formats. Swap in local spots and you have a plan.

The City Weekend

2 nights · brunch, rooftops, nightlife

Friday

  • Check in and decorate the rental
  • Welcome cocktails and charcuterie
  • Group dinner downtown
  • Rooftop bar or cocktail lounge

Saturday

  • Bottomless brunch with matching outfits
  • Walking tour, boat ride, or pedal tavern
  • Pool time and naps
  • Private chef dinner at the rental
  • Dancing or a live show

Sunday

  • Coffee and pastries
  • Recovery brunch
  • Gift opening before checkout

The Beach Getaway

3 nights · sun, boat, seafood

Thursday

  • Arrive and stock the beach house
  • Low-key seafood dinner
  • Bonfire or sunset on the sand

Friday

  • Morning beach yoga
  • Private boat or sandbar cruise
  • Fresh seafood dinner
  • Beach bar crawl

Saturday

  • Spa treatments or mobile massages
  • Bride's choice lunch
  • Themed dinner and games night
  • Private entertainment at the house

Sunday

  • Final beach walk
  • Brunch and checkout

The Relaxed & Chic

2 nights · wine, wellness, good food

Friday

  • Arrive at a cabin, lake house, or boutique hotel
  • Wine and cheese welcome
  • Dinner at a farm-to-table spot

Saturday

  • Guided hike or sunrise yoga
  • Winery or brewery tour
  • Pottery, painting, or flower-arranging class
  • Long dinner with toasts

Sunday

  • Slow breakfast
  • Group photos and goodbyes

Bachelorette Planning Favorites

For more inspiration, a few sites are worth bookmarking. Bach Babes plans and decorates full bachelorette weekends and shares plenty of destination ideas. The Knot and WeddingWire cover bachelorette themes, destinations, and etiquette alongside the rest of the wedding timeline, and Brides has solid advice on who pays, who gets invited, and how to keep the peace in a big group.

If your group is headed somewhere specific, local guides help. Party Charleston is a deep resource for bachelorette nightlife and activities in Charleston, SC, while Bach Weekend covers Nashville, New Orleans, and Miami. For a more design-forward, done-for-you weekend, Bach Me is another planning company worth a look.
