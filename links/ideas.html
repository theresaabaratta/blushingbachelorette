Party Ideas

Activities and themes that work in nearly any city or beach town

Daytime Activities

Plan one anchor activity per day and leave the rest loose. Boat cruises, pedal taverns, winery and brewery tours, and group fitness classes are easy to book for big groups. Creative classes like pottery, cocktail making, or flower arranging give everyone something to take home.

Nights Out

A great group dinner followed by a rooftop bar is the classic combo. Comedy clubs, drag brunches, karaoke rooms, and dueling piano bars are reliable crowd-pleasers. Private entertainment at the rental is a fun option for groups who want the party without the bar lines.

Themes That Photograph Well

Last Disco, Last Rodeo, coastal cowgirl, all-white with a bride in color, and "Bach and Boujee" are popular for a reason: they are easy to shop for and look great in photos. Pick one theme for the big night instead of one every day.

Low-Key Options

Not every bride wants a wild weekend. Spa days, cabin trips, cooking classes with a private chef, and slow wine-country weekends make for a relaxed celebration that still feels special.

Tip Ask the bride for her top three must-dos and build the weekend around those first.
