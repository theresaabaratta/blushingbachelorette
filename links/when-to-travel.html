When to Travel

Timing affects price, weather, and who can make it

Timing Around the Wedding

Most bachelorette parties happen one to three months before the wedding. That gives the bride breathing room and keeps the trip from colliding with final fittings and showers.

Spring

March through May is peak season in most party cities. Weather is mild and the energy is high, but rentals and flights cost more and fill fast.

Summer

Beach and lake destinations shine in summer. Expect heat and higher prices in coastal towns, and plan for afternoon storms in the South.

Fall

September through early November offers comfortable weather, harvest-season wineries, and fewer crowds once school is back in session.

Winter

Warm-weather cities and mountain cabins are the best winter options. Prices drop in many destinations, which makes it a smart choice for budget-minded groups.

Tip Avoid big local events like festivals and football weekends unless you plan to be part of them, since prices spike.
